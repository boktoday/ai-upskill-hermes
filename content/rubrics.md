# The rubrics

Every artefact in this course is judged against the same four levels, published here before you start. The
checker enforces the mechanical rules. A rubric is for the judgement a checker cannot make, and it is what the
Saturday review uses, so nobody is graded against a standard they have not seen.

**1 Novice** means the file exists and little else. **2 Developing** means the shape is there but the thinking
is borrowed or thin. **3 Proficient** means it would survive a manager reading it. **4 Exemplary** means a
stranger could use it without you in the room.

## Lesson 1: your work, task by task

| Criterion | 1 Novice | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coverage | A handful of tasks, mostly job titles | Eight or more, but whole jobs mixed with tasks | Eight to twelve real tasks from one week | A week a stranger could map onto their own work |
| Labels | Labels missing or all the same | Labels present but argued | Each row labelled with the reason it earns | Every machine row names the machine and the input |
| Human-only reasons | "It matters to me" | Reasons about preference | Reasons about what a machine cannot do | Reasons a sceptical manager would accept |
| Honesty | Aspirational tasks you would like to do | Mostly real, one or two invented | All from the last fortnight | Includes the tasks you avoid |

## Lesson 2: the rules you work under

| Criterion | 1 Novice | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Four headings | One or two headings | All four, partly copied | All four in your own words | Specific enough to settle an argument |
| Redaction | None, or a surname deleted | One category changed | Three categories changed with placeholders | The after version still makes sense to a reader |
| Employer line | Absent or vague | Quoted policy | Your reading of the policy, or the strictest reading stated | A named person you would ask |
| Verification | "I would check it" | A general habit | A step you actually run | A step that caught something |

## Lesson 3: your 90 day plan

| Criterion | 1 Novice | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dates | None | One date | Three dated commitments inside 90 days | Dates you have put in a calendar |
| Evidence | Wishes | Artefacts named but not described | Each commitment names its proof | Proof a stranger could check |
| Stop doing | Missing | Present but vague | A specific thing you will stop | Something you have already stopped |
| Cost | Depends on buying tools | Mentions free options | Costs nothing beyond your model bill | Cheaper than the time it saves |

## Lesson 4: the words that get work done

| Criterion | 1 Novice | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Terms | A few copied definitions | Twenty terms, definitions only | Twenty terms each with a Say line in your job | The glossary you would hand a new colleague |
| Say lines | Missing | Generic sentences | Concrete sentences naming your own work | Sentences you have said out loud |
| Trouble phrases | None | Listed | Five phrases each with a repair | Repairs you have tested |
| Range | Only chat terms | Chat and prompts | Chat, prompt, agent and guardrail terms | Covers the terms your industry argues about |

## Lesson 5: what a skill actually is

| Criterion | 1 Novice | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Install | Read about skills | Installed one, untested | Installed and triggered | Installed two and know why each routes |
| Evidence | Described | One test pasted | Both tests pasted with outcomes | The transcript shows the failure too |
| Routing | Cannot explain | Guesses | Names the words in the description that routed | Rewrote the description to change the routing |
| Secrets | A key in the file | A key mentioned | Key location named, value absent | Key in a manager, documented |

## Lesson 6: teach your agent once

| Criterion | 1 Novice | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Description | Missing or vague | Present but describes rather than triggers | Trigger first and short enough to route | Routes on two different phrasings |
| Steps | Prose | Mostly prose with some commands | Imperative steps in order | Steps a stranger could follow without asking |
| Check | None | "It looked right" | A runnable check | A check that failed once and was fixed |
| Failure | Not attempted | Noticed but unexplained | One real failure found and fixed | The fix is recorded with what it taught you |

## Lesson 7: your first mini app

| Criterion | 1 Novice | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Live | Runs on your machine only | Deployed but broken | Loads with no console errors | Loads in under two seconds |
| Phone | Horizontal scrolling | Fits but cramped | Usable at 390 pixels | Designed for the phone first |
| Task | A demo of a form | Half a task | One real task completed | Somebody else used it for that task |
| Ownership | No name | A name | Name and purpose on the page | A way to report a bug |

## Lesson 8: an agent that does a chore

| Criterion | 1 Novice | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Runs | Only inside the chat | A script that needs tweaking | One command from a clean terminal | Runs on a machine that is not yours |
| Guardrail | A comment | A warning | A human step before anything leaves | The step is logged |
| Log | Rehearsal | One line | A real run with time and outcome | Several runs showing what changed |
| Credentials | Key in the folder | Key in a file nearby | Location named, value absent | Key scoped and rotated |

<!-- lesson-09 -->
## Lesson 9: your portfolio

| Criterion | 1 Novice | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Links | None that load | One working link | Every card links to something that loads | Every link still loads a month later |
| Wording | Adjectives | Mixed first and third person | First person, no adjectives | Reads like you speak |
| Dates | Missing | One date | A date on every card | Dates that show progress |
| Phone | Broken | Scrolls sideways | Works at phone width | Looks deliberate on a phone |

<!-- lesson-10 -->
## Lesson 10: what an API is, and what it is not

| Criterion | 1 Novice | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Calls | Documentation quoted | One real call | Two real calls with real status codes | A call you built into something |
| Errors | None | An error mentioned | One failure with its cause | A failure you diagnosed by reading the code |
| Permission | Cannot say | Vague | Names which part is the key and which is the address | Explains scope in plain words |
| Secrets | Key in the file | Key redacted by hand | Key location named, never the value | Key never touched your notes |

## Lesson 11: connected tools and webhooks

| Criterion | 1 Novice | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Event | Screenshot of docs | One event, no body | A real event with its payload | A second event proving the shape |
| Scope | Unknown | Named connection | Connection and scope recorded | Scope reduced after review |
| Fields | None identified | One field | Two meaningful fields and one noise field | A field you were wrong about |
| Safety | A trigger that sends | Destructive trigger with a warning | Nothing destructive without a human step | A test that proves it cannot fire |

## Lesson 12: clocks, what a cronjob is

| Criterion | 1 Novice | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Schedule | "Every morning" | Five fields, no timezone | Five fields, plain English, timezone named | Handles daylight saving |
| Runs | None | One manual run | One manual and one on time | A week of runs |
| Failure | Unnoticed | Recorded | Recorded with the fix that makes it speak | Alerted you before you noticed |
| Idempotence | Duplicates output | Unknown | Safe to run twice | Proven safe with a second run |

## Lesson 13: verify before you trust

| Criterion | 1 Novice | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coverage | One claim checked | Several, chosen for luck | Every claim on the portfolio has a row | Rows added before the claims were made |
| Repeatable | "Reviewed by me" | A description of a check | A step another person could run | Somebody else ran it |
| Failures | None recorded | An honest miss | At least one failure recorded | The failure changed how you work |
| Match | Log and page disagree | Mostly agree | Page updated to match the log | The log is public |

## Lesson 14: failure drills

| Criterion | 1 Novice | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Drills | Read about failures | One caused | Three caused, with symptom, cause and check | A fourth you invented |
| Causation | Imagined | Half caused | Caused and reproducible | Caused on somebody else's machine |
| Fixes | Missing | Suggested | Written beside each drill | Fixed and re-tested |
| Safety | Harmed something real | Came close | Nothing real touched | A sandbox you built first |

## Lesson 15: ship and tell

| Criterion | 1 Novice | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Exemplary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Claims | Adjectives | Claims with some support | Every claim supported by a link | A sceptic checked and agreed |
| The cannot line | Missing or a boast | Present but vague | Specific and honest | It cost you something to write |
| Length | Under 100 or over 400 words | Close to the range | 180 to 220 words | Every sentence earns its place |
| Placement | In a file | On the portfolio | Live and linked | Sent to a person who replied |
