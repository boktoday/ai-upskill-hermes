# The rubrics

Every artefact in this course is judged against the same four levels, published here before you start. The checker enforces the mechanical rules. A rubric is for the judgement a checker cannot make, and it is what the Saturday review uses, so nobody is graded against a standard they have not seen.

1 Novice means the file exists and little else. 2 Developing means the shape is there but the thinking is borrowed or thin. 3 Proficient means it would survive a manager reading it. 4 Exemplary means a stranger could use it without you in the room.
