How it runs

  1. You open the lesson page and read it. It takes two minutes, not twenty.
  2. The agent builds the demo in front of you, in your own folder, and says why each choice was made.
  3. You do the same job on your own work. The page tells you exactly what to hand in.
  4. The agent runs the checks against your file and shows you the output. Pass, and the lesson is sealed.
  5. Between lessons, short reviews keep the earlier ones from fading.

The rule that makes this different

Nothing is marked complete because it sounds finished. The agent opens the file, runs the check and quotes the result. If your page does not load, the lesson is not passed, and it says so plainly.
